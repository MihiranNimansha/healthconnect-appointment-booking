Medical Specialties

From heart health to mental wellness — explore our full network of trusted specialist doctors, and find the right care for every need.

Cardiology

3 Doctors Available

Cardiology is the branch of medicine that deals with disorders of the heart and the cardiovascular system. Our cardiologists are experts in diagnosing, treating, and preventing heart disease — from high blood pressure to complex heart conditions.

Common Conditions

  • Coronary Artery Disease
  • Heart Failure
  • Arrhythmia
  • Hypertension
  • Valvular Heart Disease

What to Expect

  • ECG & Stress Testing
  • Echocardiography
  • Risk Factor Assessment
  • Medication Management
  • Lifestyle Counselling

Dermatology

2 Doctors Available

Dermatology focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the skin, hair, and nails. Our dermatologists provide care for everything from acne and eczema to complex skin cancers, using the latest evidence-based therapies.

Common Conditions

  • Acne & Rosacea
  • Eczema & Psoriasis
  • Skin Cancer Screening
  • Alopecia (Hair Loss)
  • Fungal Infections

What to Expect

  • Skin Biopsy & Histology
  • Phototherapy (UV Treatment)
  • Topical & Oral Medications
  • Dermatoscopy
  • Cosmetic Procedures

Pediatrics

2 Doctors Available

Pediatrics is dedicated to the health and well-being of children from birth through adolescence. Our pediatricians offer compassionate, family-centred care combining preventive medicine, early developmental tracking, and management of childhood illnesses.

Common Conditions

  • Asthma & Allergies
  • Developmental Delays
  • Infectious Diseases
  • Nutritional Disorders
  • ADHD & Behavioural Issues

What to Expect

  • Routine Vaccinations
  • Growth & Development Review
  • Newborn Screening
  • School Health Assessments
  • Parent Counselling

Orthopedics

1 Doctor Available

Orthopedics specialises in the diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of disorders and injuries affecting the musculoskeletal system — including bones, joints, muscles, tendons, and ligaments. Our surgeons and specialists help patients regain mobility and quality of life.

Common Conditions

  • Fractures & Dislocations
  • Arthritis & Joint Pain
  • Sports Injuries
  • Spinal Disorders
  • Tendon & Ligament Tears

What to Expect

  • X-Ray & MRI Imaging
  • Physical Therapy Referrals
  • Joint Replacement Surgery
  • Arthroscopic Procedures
  • Cast & Splint Management

Neurology

1 Doctor Available

Neurology is the branch of medicine concerned with the study and treatment of disorders of the nervous system — encompassing the brain, spinal cord, and all peripheral nerves. Our neurologists offer expert evaluation for complex neurological conditions.

Common Conditions

  • Epilepsy & Seizures
  • Migraines & Headaches
  • Multiple Sclerosis
  • Parkinson's Disease
  • Stroke & TIA

What to Expect

  • EEG & Brain Imaging (MRI)
  • Nerve Conduction Studies
  • Lumbar Puncture
  • Medication Optimisation
  • Cognitive Assessments
Find Neurologists

General Practice

1 Doctor Available

General Practitioners (GPs) are your first point of contact for any health concern. They provide comprehensive, continuous care for patients of all ages, managing a wide range of conditions and coordinating referrals to specialists when needed.

Common Services

  • Annual Health Check-Ups
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Minor Ailments & Infections
  • Family Planning
  • Mental Health First Step

What to Expect

  • Full Medical History Review
  • Blood Tests & Screening
  • Prescriptions & Referrals
  • Preventive Care Advice
  • Vaccination Management
